Super-dj — Privacy Policy

Super-dj is a personal-use live-streaming tool. This page describes what it does with your data.

What Super-dj stores

An account (email + password hash), uploaded audio tracks and cover images, playlists, overlay templates, and configured stream destinations (RTMP URLs and stream keys, encrypted at rest).

Google / YouTube access

When you connect a YouTube destination, Super-dj requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube scope to create and manage a live broadcast and live stream on your own YouTube channel on your behalf, for the duration of your streaming session. Super-dj stores an encrypted OAuth refresh token for this purpose. It does not read your other YouTube data, and does not share any Google account data with third parties.

Data sharing

Super-dj does not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is used solely to operate the service for its account holder.
